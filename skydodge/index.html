Privacy Policy for Sky Dodge

Effective Date: September 8, 2026

Developer: 9K Lab

Sky Dodge ("the Game") is developed and published by 9K Lab. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information handled during your gameplay experience. This Privacy Policy explains what data the Game processes and how it is protected.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We believe in user privacy and minimal data exposure. Sky Dodge does not collect, harvest, or store any sensitive Personal Identifiable Information (PII), such as:

  • Real name, home address, or phone number
  • Email address
  • Financial or payment card details
  • Device location (GPS or precise location)
  • Contacts, camera, microphone, or external storage files

2. Information Processed for Game Features

To provide global arcade leaderboards and preserve game progress, the Game processes the following non-sensitive data:

  • Leaderboard Nickname & High Scores: When you submit your score to the global leaderboard, your chosen in-game pilot callsign (nickname) and score are transmitted to our leaderboard service (LootLocker).
  • Anonymous Game Account ID: The Game uses an anonymous guest identifier generated by LootLocker to associate your high score with your device installation. No personal account registration or login is required.
  • Local Game Data: Game settings, unlocked achievements, best scores, and sound volume preferences are stored locally on your device storage (sandboxed app storage).

3. Third-Party Services

Sky Dodge uses the following third-party service solely for gameplay ranking functionality:

  • LootLocker: Used to manage global leaderboard rankings and guest session verification. You can review LootLocker's privacy policy at https://lootlocker.com/privacy-policy.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Sky Dodge does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. The game does not contain behavioral advertising, third-party user tracking SDKs, or social network integrations. If a parent or guardian believes their child has submitted personal information, please contact us immediately and we will promptly remove it.

5. Security of Data

All network communications between the Game and leaderboard servers use industry-standard HTTPS/TLS encryption. Local save data is stored within Android's protected application sandbox.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in functionality or legal requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
